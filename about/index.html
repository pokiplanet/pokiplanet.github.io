About PokiPlanet

PokiPlanet is a hobby project run by a long-time Pokémon TCG enthusiast and researcher.

With over 10 years of collecting experience, They bring a deep passion for Pokémon TCG history, collecting, and community.

You might recognize the alias pikachutcg from the EliteFourum (efour) community, a platform where they have contributed to discussions around rare cards and collector insights.

Here on PokiPlanet, you'll find explorations of Pokémon TCG history, collection strategies, research-based insights, and blog articles designed to help fellow collectors.
